CHILDREN WORLD - BUILD PACK 15 v0.1 CHANGELOG
12 September 2026

CORRECTION 1 - MOBILE HORIZONTAL OVERFLOW
- Constrained the root document to the viewport.
- Removed the viewport-width decorative pseudo-element that could create scrollable overflow.
- Added min-width:0/flexible grid containment to the main landing-page structures.
- Clipped decorative hero/orbit overflow at its owning section instead of allowing it to expand the page.
- Kept mobile portrait and mobile landscape as separate protected responsive modes.

CORRECTION 2 - PUBLIC DEMO STATUS
- Public Demo is now a non-navigation status chip.
- Compact menu Public Demo state is also informational only.
- Start Family Trial stays a separate explicit action.

CORRECTION 3 - BUILD YOUR CITY DIFFICULTY
- Kept the GBP 2,350,000 starting budget.
- Mission now requires: 5,000+ population, all essential services, Education 50+, and GBP 30k-GBP 70k remaining.
- Adviser gives targeted hints when population, services, education or reserve are not balanced.
- This prevents the mission from ending after simply placing one of each obvious service.

CORRECTION 4 - BRAIN BATTLE LANGUAGE SWITCH
- Active Brain Battle run is saved locally.
- EN/FR switching restores current tier, question number, score, streak, missed-question list and answered-state feedback.
- No restart to the Brain Battle homepage during an active run.

CORRECTION 5 - BRAIN BATTLE MASTER ENDING
- Added a second completion action: Start Again from Explorer.
- Kept Play Master Mission Again as the top-tier replay option.

CORRECTION 6 - CREATOR STUDIO OPEN MISSION
- Replaced the old selection-only workbench with a real structured mission workspace.
- Each mission now opens planning prompts, selectable design features, project notes and saved draft state.
- Added Save & continue, Clear this draft and Mission list controls.

CORRECTION 7 - FLAG QUEST WRONG-ANSWER FEEDBACK
- Incorrect answer and timeout feedback no longer auto-disappear.
- Correct answer remains visible until the child presses Next question.
- Correct responses still move at normal game pace.

AUDIT
- Functionality Audit updated to Build Pack 15 and reflects these behaviours.
