Children World — Future Vehicle Fix 27
Current batch: Problems 1–2
Date: 2026-09-20

Problem 1 — Vehicle blueprint/body identity
- Strengthened the blueprint silhouette for every vehicle family so the vehicle is recognisable from the blueprint itself.
- Sport Car remains low and aerodynamic.
- 4×4 / SUV now has a taller, boxier body, flatter roof, raised stance and larger wheels in its finished render.
- Van now has a short bonnet, tall passenger/cargo box, sliding-door/body divisions and tall roof structure.
- Intercity Coach now has a long high passenger body, repeated window bays, luggage/belt line and coach proportions.
- Farm Tractor now has a separate low engine bonnet, tall driver cab and agricultural proportions.
- Switching to Van / Coach / Tractor now forces a fresh render of that vehicle's own blueprint instead of leaving a previous vehicle's final-test canvas visible.

Problem 2 — Test / Mission Complete state leaking between vehicles
- Test, reveal, celebration and Mission Complete presentation are now reset whenever the user changes vehicle.
- The fitted puzzle work is NOT erased; it remains stored separately in vehicleBuildStates for each vehicle.
- A completed SUV can no longer leave its Mission Complete screen on the Sport Car, Van, Coach or Tractor.
- When returning to a vehicle, its own fitted parts, mode, powertrain, Future Tech, paint, name and progress are restored.
- Test My Creation is recalculated from the selected vehicle's own build only.
- If that selected vehicle is complete, it receives its own persistent Ready to Test notice; if incomplete, stale result text is cleared.

No other Creator Studio mission was changed.
