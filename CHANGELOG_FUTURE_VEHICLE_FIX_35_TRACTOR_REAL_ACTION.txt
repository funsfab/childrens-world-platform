Children World — Future Vehicle Fix 35
Tractor real-action animation rewrite

Files included
- js/creator.js
- CHANGELOG_FUTURE_VEHICLE_FIX_35_TRACTOR_REAL_ACTION.txt

Main changes
1. Farm field ground is now earth/brown soil instead of a flat green floor.
2. Mowing now uses a front-mounted mower, tall grass ahead, short stubble behind, spinning mower blades and flying grass clippings.
3. Ploughing now shows lighter unworked soil, darker turned soil, furrows, animated dust and soil clods physically lifting and moving behind the plough.
4. Precision planting now shows live seed streams dropping from the seeder into the soil plus planted rows remaining behind.
5. Irrigation now uses continuously moving water droplets, arcs and landing splashes with visibly darker wet soil behind the tractor.
6. Loader/levelling now shows moving soil particles and a shrinking mound while the levelled surface grows.
7. Smart-farm/drone phase now uses brown soil with green crop rows rather than a flat green background.
8. Drone rotors and scan sweep are continuously animated.
9. Targeted irrigation and crop-support phases now show moving spray/mist rather than static graphics.
10. Farm people/rest area remain visible through more stages and change positions/actions between phases.
11. Girl rest-area cup now moves toward her mouth so the drinking action is visible.
12. Tractor tool animation is independent of wheel spin; mower, seeder, irrigator and loader have their own motion.
13. Left-moving precision phase wheel direction is corrected to match travel direction.

This update keeps the existing file path js/creator.js so it replaces the previous JavaScript file rather than adding a duplicate.
