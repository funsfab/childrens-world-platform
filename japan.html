CULTURE & FOOD

Tradition and modern life can exist side by side.

🍜Food

Examples include ramen, sushi, rice dishes and many regional specialities.

🎎Traditions

Festivals and customs vary by region and family; avoid thinking one tradition represents everyone.

🎮Entertainment

Japan has major video-game, animation, music and publishing industries.

🚅Transport

Rail is important in many cities, and Shinkansen trains connect major regions at high speed.

🌏 Culture rule: We describe what is popular, traditional or common without pretending every person lives the same way.
