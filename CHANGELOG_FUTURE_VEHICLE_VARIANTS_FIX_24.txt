Children World — Future Vehicle Multi-Vehicle Build Fix 24
Date: 2026-09-19

Purpose
-------
Add the four remaining Future Vehicle build types without copying the Sport Car final animation.
The Sport Car remains the only vehicle connected to the existing final transformation/test sequence.

Added vehicle builds
--------------------
1. 4×4 / SUV
2. Van
3. Intercity Coach / Bus
4. Farm Tractor

What now works for every vehicle
--------------------------------
- Vehicle can be selected from the compact vehicle list.
- Each vehicle has its own preview image and its own 3D-style blueprint proportions.
- Left / Right / Front / Rear / Top / Interior / Free rotate views continue to work.
- Body & Movement uses vehicle-specific puzzle locations and quantities.
- Power System supports Petrol, Diesel, Electric and Hybrid choices.
- Safety uses matching cabin/exterior slots and quantities for the selected vehicle.
- Future Tech supports Flying Car and Water / Amphibious paths, including Propeller / Jet / Drone Lift.
- Part buttons lock after every required copy has been fitted.
- Completed build sections receive the normal completion state and auto-progress behaviour.
- Test My Creation stays locked until Body & Movement, Power, Safety and Future Tech are all complete.

Vehicle-specific differences
----------------------------
4×4 / SUV
- Taller body and higher ground-clearance blueprint.
- Four doors, four side windows, four road wheels and multi-seat interior.

Van
- Taller/longer body.
- Front + sliding-door style puzzle locations.
- More side windows and passenger seats.

Intercity Coach / Bus
- Long coach body.
- Six road wheels / three axles.
- Repeated coach passenger windows.
- Passenger doors and a larger multi-row seating puzzle.
- Rear-oriented power-system positioning where appropriate.

Farm Tractor
- Tractor body/cabin silhouette.
- Smaller front wheels and much larger rear wheels.
- Cabin doors/windows, engine bonnet and single-driver interior.
- Tractor-appropriate body puzzle omits a normal passenger-car boot/trunk.

Vehicle switching
-----------------
- Switching between vehicles no longer carries fitted pieces into another vehicle.
- During the current browser session, each vehicle build is cached separately.
- Save Project stores those per-vehicle prototype build states so they can be restored later on the same browser/device.

Final animation rule
--------------------
- Sport Car: existing full transformation, paint, Test World, systems test and Mission Complete sequence is unchanged.
- 4×4 / SUV, Van, Intercity Coach and Farm Tractor: once all four build areas are complete, Test My Creation unlocks and confirms that the build is complete, but DOES NOT reuse the Sport Car final animation.
- Their individual final animations/test journeys are intentionally left for the next design decision.
