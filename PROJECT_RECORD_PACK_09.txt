PACK 09 PROJECT RECORD — APPROVED DIRECTION

- Fun is equal to learning.
- Children World must support Learn + Play + Create + Build + Watch + Explore + Life Skills.
- The platform must feel premium, lively and age-appropriate for 10–13, not babyish and not like homework-only software.
- Build Your City becomes a flagship drag-and-drop strategy game that grows over time.
- Story World is a flagship 3D cinematic mini-film area and must be visible from My World HQ.
- The Arcade should eventually contain genuinely fun replayable games as well as educational games.
- Questions must become deeper, adaptive and non-repetitive, with very large reviewed banks in production.
- Challenge progression: Explorer → Challenger → Investigator → Expert → Master Mission.
- Prove It reasoning should sometimes require children to explain why an answer is correct.
- Future no-code Owner/Admin Studio will let the owner publish content without editing code.
- Parent Centre will include private feedback to Admin.
- UK-first, whole-world content, globally expandable and multilingual from the architecture stage.
- School books are references, not the limit: Children World uses a blended curriculum with carefully controlled stretch content beyond age 13 where appropriate.
