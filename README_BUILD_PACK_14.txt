MAJOR CHANGES INCLUDED
1. Brain Battle rebuilt around fixed progression: Explorer → Challenger → Investigator → Expert → Master Mission.
2. Each Brain Battle level now contains 20 questions (100 total in this build).
3. Questions are no longer deliberately mixed between tiers inside one level.
4. A child must finish the current level before progressing to the next.
5. Incorrect Brain Battle questions are stored for a review round.
6. Missed questions must be answered correctly before the level is mastered.
7. Completion messages now match the level actually completed.
8. Brain Battle progress/reset controls added for repeat testing.
9. Build Your City starting budget raised so the balanced-city mission is achievable.
10. Build Your City now requires 5 homes plus school, hospital, park, fire station, bus hub and shops.
11. The city mission does NOT require spending the budget down to £0.
12. City mission completion now explains the remaining budget clearly.
13. Build Your City mobile portrait layout received dedicated responsive treatment.
14. Life Lab item controls now support both adding and removing basket items.
15. Flag Quest now shows actual country flags beneath country codes instead of text-only FLAG placeholders.
16. Flag Quest uses more relevant real-world visual artwork for world/capital/geography questions.
17. Flag Quest sets increased from 10 to 20 questions.
18. Flag Question banks expanded to 40 Flags, 40 Capitals and 40 Mixed World questions in this build.
19. Recently used Flag Quest questions are avoided until the pool needs refreshing.
20. Quest completion now offers Play another set, Choose another category, and Back to HQ flows.
21. A World Explorer landing page was added so Japan is treated as one country mission, not the whole World Explorer.
22. Navigation labels now use World Explorer instead of World Explorer — Japan where appropriate.
23. English/French language switching was added across the child-facing prototype pages.
24. A persistent background-music controller was added with mute/unmute and automatic track rotation.
25. Thirty original prototype background tracks are included locally; these are not commercial copyrighted songs.
26. Homepage/header responsive behaviour was refined across mobile portrait, mobile landscape, tablet portrait, tablet landscape and desktop.
27. The oversized locked-content treatment was reduced so card information stays readable.
28. Homepage access/navigation structure was simplified to avoid duplicate/confusing trial actions.
29. Creator Studio retains the child-led mission concept: children create and save their own idea; it is not an admin request form.
30. Functionality Audit was updated to reflect the new working/partial systems.

MUSIC NOTE
The included audio files are original prototype tracks generated for testing the background-music system. Popular commercial songs such as “Heal the World” are not included. Commercial music licensing can be handled later if the project chooses that route.

LANGUAGE NOTE
This is the first EN/FR prototype implementation. French is available across the reviewed child-facing pages. Parent/Admin areas will receive their full internationalisation pass when those areas are reviewed.

PROTOTYPE / PRODUCTION NOTE
This remains a browser/localStorage prototype. It is not yet the final secure production backend. Accounts, subscriptions, permanent cloud progress, production analytics and database-enforced access still require the later backend phase.
