Future Vehicle — Fix 28
Current batch: Problem 1

Problem fixed
-------------
Completing Future Tech could make the builder think Body & Movement / Power / Safety
were incomplete again. The interface would jump back to Body & Movement and keep
Test My Creation disabled even though the child had already fitted those sections.

Cause
-----
When a saved Power-System or Future-Tech alternative was restored, the shared
restoreObjects() function cleared the entire vehicle object layer before restoring
that one variant. That removed fitted pieces belonging to the other completed
vehicle systems.

Correction
----------
- Saved Power-System variants now restore by appending only their own pieces.
- Saved Future-Tech variants now restore by appending only their own pieces.
- Full vehicle/project restores still replace the entire object layer normally.
- Body & Movement, Power, Safety and Future Tech can therefore coexist correctly.
- When all four areas are complete, Test My Creation remains enabled instead of
  automatically returning to Body & Movement.
- This applies to Sport Car, 4x4/SUV, Van, Coach and Tractor.

No unrelated visual/animation changes were made in this fix.
