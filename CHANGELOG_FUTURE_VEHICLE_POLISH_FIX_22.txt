CHILDREN WORLD — FUTURE VEHICLE POLISH FIX 22
Date: 19 September 2026
Baseline: Master copy after Future Vehicle Phase 5 / Fix 21

This build preserves the completed Sport Car mission and applies the agreed Future Vehicle cleanup corrections.

CORRECTION 1 — BLUEPRINT / DRAWING TOOL SEPARATION
- Added the Blueprint On/Off control to Vehicle Blueprint Views.
- When Blueprint is ON (default), the large drawing toolbar is hidden.
- When Blueprint is OFF, creative drawing tools appear.
- Returning Blueprint ON automatically returns to Select/build mode.

CORRECTION 2 — AUTOMATIC BUILD-STAGE PROGRESSION
- Completed build areas receive a clear Complete check.
- After the final required piece of the current area is fitted, the next unfinished area opens automatically.
- Children can still manually select any unfinished/completed area.
- Progression skips areas already completed.

CORRECTION 3 — TEST BUTTON LOCK
- Test My Creation stays visible but disabled until Body & Movement, Power System, Safety and Future Tech are all complete.
- Optional pieces such as Solar Assist do not block testing.
- When all required areas are complete, Test My Creation unlocks and briefly highlights.

CORRECTION 4 — COMPACT VEHICLE SELECTOR
- Removed the large Executive Sedan card from Future Vehicle.
- Future Vehicle now shows one large vehicle preview plus a compact vehicle list.
- Sport Car is the current working vehicle.
- 4x4/SUV, Van, Intercity Coach/Bus and Farm Tractor are reserved as coming vehicles without lengthening the page.

CORRECTION 5 — OPTIONAL NOTES SIMPLIFIED
- Removed “Who is it for?” and “What problem does it solve?” from Future Vehicle.
- Kept one optional notes field only, inside the existing collapsed Notes and Decisions area.

CORRECTION 6 — VEHICLE LEARNING / QUIZ
- Added “Test What You Learned” after Mission Complete.
- Opens a randomized five-question Vehicle Knowledge Challenge.
- Questions cover car parts, safety, selected power system, selected Future Tech and why vehicle types are engineered differently.
- Each answer gives a short learning explanation and score.

CORRECTION 7 — CONTEXT-SENSITIVE BLUEPRINT INSTRUCTIONS
- Blueprint guidance now redraws immediately whenever Body, Power, Safety or Future Tech is selected.
- Added correct instructions before/after Power and Future Tech choices.
- Safety instructions no longer remain visible after switching to Future Tech.

CORRECTION 8 — CHILD-FACING STAGE FEEDBACK
- Removed visible Phase 1/2/3/4/5 numbering from the child experience.
- Added compact floating mission-status messages near the vehicle area.
- Colour stage includes a quick action that scrolls to the colour controls on small screens.
- Existing transition sounds remain part of the feedback.

CORRECTION 9 — DEVELOPMENT LANGUAGE
- Child-facing flow no longer uses phase/prototype wording.
- Browser/server-saving reminder is now shown only in Owner/Admin Preview.
- Internal code may still use phase names for engineering purposes.

CORRECTION 10 — AMPHIBIOUS WATER TRANSITION
- Reworked road-to-water transition into visible road -> shoreline -> launch ramp -> water -> open-water travel.
- Added water surface, shoreline/ramp, buoys and clearer water-zone scenery.
- Wheels retract progressively after entering the water.
- Full Systems Test begins in open water and the return shoreline/ramp appears only as the car comes back.

CORRECTION 11 — MISSION COMPLETE REDESIGN
- Removed duplicated “Future Vehicle Engineer — Mission Complete” badge text.
- Keeps one strong Mission Complete / Future Vehicle Engineer heading.
- Completion badges were moved lower so they do not cover the car or illuminated platform.
- Achievement wording is cleaner and the final vehicle remains the visual focus.

CORRECTION 12 — TEST LAB LABEL
- Removed the “Test Lab” label from the Future Test World.
- The scenery can remain, but only meaningful route/location labels are shown.

ADDITIONAL ADMIN REQUIREMENT — COMPLIMENTARY ACCESS
- Added an Owner/Admin “Complimentary Access” manager.
- Owner can create Family, Tester or Staff complimentary access records with optional expiry and admin notes.
- Records can be suspended, reactivated or removed.
- “Use on this device” applies Full World access without payment for prototype testing.
- This is local prototype administration; production account invitations and cross-device access still require the final backend/account system.
